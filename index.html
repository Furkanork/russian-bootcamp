Comrade, sprint from A2 toward B1 in eight weeks

A study sprint built on the 80/20 rule: the most common words, three cases, verb aspect and ready-made phrases, used out loud every day. It takes about 75 minutes a day, around 70 hours in total. That builds the core that does most of the work; reaching a full B1 usually takes more practice after week 8, and the reviews and readings keep going.

Манифест 80/20

Learn the 20% first. The other 80% can wait.

At B1 you can handle daily life, give your opinion and tell a story about the past. These six blocks get you most of the way there. Everything on the right can wait until after B1.

The high-yield 20%

  • 1
    The most frequent 2,000 wordsRanked by real frequency in the word list below. Together they make up about 86% of everyday spoken Russian: the 80/20 rule at work.
  • 2
    Prepositional, accusative, genitiveWhere? Where to? Of / not / have. Most of the case endings you produce come from these three.
  • 3
    Verb aspect in past and futureчитал (process, habit) vs прочитал (done, result). This is the biggest step from A2 to B1.
  • 4
    Six motion verbsидти/ходить, ехать/ездить, нести/носить plus prefixes при-, у-, вы-, за-, по-.
  • 5
    Linking wordsпотому что, поэтому, который, если, чтобы, хотя turn sentences into speech.
  • 6
    50 ready-made chunksMemorise whole phrases like «Мне кажется, что…» instead of building them.

The 80% you can postpone

  • Later
    Participles and gerundsRecognise читающий, прочитав when reading. Don't produce them yet.
  • Later
    Every motion-verb prefixПере-, об-, раз-…ся can wait. Five prefixes carry daily speech.
  • Later
    Rare plural endingsLearn the genitive plurals you meet constantly: лет, людей, раз, денег, минут.
  • Later
    Literary and bureaucratic vocabularyNews and classics come after conversation feels comfortable.
  • Later
    A perfect accentGet word stress right. Your vowel reduction will improve with listening time.
  • Later
    Cursive handwritingOnly if your exam or school asks for it. Type and read print.

Stress is freeIt can fall on any syllable and changes vowels. Learn every word with its stress mark: молоко́, го́род, вода́.
Unstressed О → «а»молоко́ sounds like «малако́», хорошо́ like «харашо́». This one rule fixes most pronunciation.
Unstressed Е, Я → «и»сейча́с ≈ «сичас», язы́к ≈ «изык», тепе́рь ≈ «типерь».
Final consonants go voicelessхлеб → «хлеп», го́род → «го́рат», друг → «друк».
Ь softensThe soft sign makes the consonant before it soft: брат vs брать, мат vs мать.
Ё is always stressedIt is often printed as Е. If you see ё, you know where the stress is: ещё, всё, идёт.
